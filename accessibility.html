Station accessibility guide

Use these symbols to understand what each station access level means when you plan a journey.

Understanding accessibility symbols

♿ Full step-free access

Complete step-free access from street to platform:

  • Level access from street to ticket hall
  • Lifts or ramps to all platforms
  • Suitable for wheelchair users
  • No steps required for the main route

↔️ Interchange access

Step-free access between platforms, but street access may still involve steps:

  • Easier transfers between lines
  • Level access between platforms
  • Staff assistance may still help
  • Entrance routes can include steps

⚡ Partial step-free access

Some step-free access, but not to every platform or exit:

  • Some platforms are step-free
  • Some routes may still require steps
  • Check platform-specific details before travel
  • Plan an alternative if needed

⚠️ No step-free access

These stations are likely to present barriers for step-free travel:

  • Steps between street and platforms
  • No full lift access available
  • Not suitable as a wheelchair destination
  • Use an accessible hub and transfer instead

Before you travel

  • Check TfL live updates for lift availability
  • Staff assistance is available at stations
  • Some stations need advance notice for ramp help
  • Platform gap fillers are available at many stations
